ABOUT

Franka Emika Panda loaded from the real MuJoCo Menagerie MJCF (11 bodies, 9 dof, inertials, joint limits, per-joint torque limits 87/12 Nm). Each CEM iteration samples 32 knot-parameterized trajectories and rolls every one out through full forward dynamics - mass matrix, Newton-Euler bias, gravity-feedforward PD - in compiled Rust/WebAssembly. The best trajectory replays on the live sim with its end-effector trail.
